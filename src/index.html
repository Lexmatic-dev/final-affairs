Our purpose

What is Final Affairs?

When someone passes away, the person left in charge is handed a long list: accounts to close, institutions to notify, papers to find, decisions to make. Some of it is administrative, some of it is legal, and much of it falls somewhere in between. Most people face this only rarely, with no map.

Final Affairs is that map: one centralized, secure platform that brings together specialized support from trusted providers to simplify a loved one’s final affairs. It builds a personalized Final Affairs Wind-Down Plan™ for your situation and guides you step by step until the plan is complete. Your plan is prepared for you, then reviewed and approved by your online attorney, who also answers your questions along the way. And when a matter calls for separate legal services or legal counsel, such as probate or an estate plan, we connect you with independent probate and estate attorneys.

Why it matters

What happens if it just waits?

It is tempting to set the paperwork aside, and that is understandable, however:

  • •Accounts stay open
  • •Bills keep arriving
  • •Property sits unprotected
  • •Matters quietly grow harder to resolve as months pass

A little order now spares your family bigger problems later. That is what we’re here for.

Guidance, not guesswork.

The platform tells you what to do and in what order. Your plan is built from attorney-approved guidance for your state, your questions get plain answers, and independent local attorneys are available when you want advice or legal services. We built Final Affairs so that no one has to figure this out alone. Ready to begin? Log in with the access code from your funeral home, or contact us and we will get you started.

Planning ahead? Final Affairs Planning puts your affairs in order now and eases the burden on your future personal representative, the person who will one day handle them.
