The reality

The challenge for funeral homes

Funeral homes care for families at their most vulnerable moment, and help those who come in early to plan ahead. But after the service, families are left with a long list of tasks to settle their loved one’s final affairs. And families who plan ahead want to know that nothing will be left undone. Final Affairs gives funeral homes a way to serve both.

The partnership

What partnership means

Care for your families before and after the service, with minimal effort from your team. Final Affairs moves those tasks and questions away from your staff, so your team is never asked to answer questions, legal or otherwise, that fall outside their role.

Care that continues

Families remember who helped them when it mattered, before a loss or after it. Offering Final Affairs extends your care into planning ahead and into the months that follow a service.

Simple for your team

Introducing Final Affairs takes only a moment and asks nothing more of your staff.

Good for your business

Partnership with Final Affairs is a meaningful opportunity on both sides of your business, at-need and pre-need. We’d rather show you than tell the whole internet. Reach out and we will walk you through exactly how it works.
