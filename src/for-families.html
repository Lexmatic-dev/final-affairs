Guidance before and after a loss

After a loss, there is so much to handle.

Final Affairs helps you navigate the practical, administrative, and legal steps needed to put your loved one’s affairs in order. You can work at your own comfortable pace, so you have time to focus on what matters most. You don’t have to guess or go it alone. We were created for this sole purpose: to help you complete your own or a loved one’s final affairs.

What we do for you

In plain words

Your loved one was unique. Their life was unique. Their final affairs are just as unique. Through Final Affairs Assistance™, we create a Final Affairs Wind-Down Plan™ customized to your loved one’s specific situation. Then you work through the plan at your own pace, with professionals when needed.

One

We identify and document

We help you identify and document the estate: the accounts, property, papers, and details that matter.

Two

Your Wind-Down Plan

We build your personalized Wind-Down Plan: a clear, step-by-step guide of what to handle, made for your situation from attorney-approved guidance for your state, ready to follow from day one.

Three

We walk every step together

We guide you through each step with clear instructions and answer questions as they come up.

Four

Evaluate Probate

We help you find out whether probate is required at all, and if it is, whether simplified probate is an option or the standard process applies, and which assets may pass outside probate. An independent attorney can review the facts with you and confirm the answer in plain words

Five

Plain answers, and a trusted attorney

Your questions are gathered in one place so nothing is missed, and when legal services are needed, such as probate or an estate plan, we connect you with an independent local attorney experienced in the services you need. Everything you have gathered, documents, details, and questions, transfers to your attorney in one organized file, so no time is lost and no effort is duplicated getting them up to speed.

01 Is Final Affairs a law firm?

No. Final Affairs is a technology and support platform. Our software and support team keep everything organized and moving, and your plan is built from questions, guidance, and instructions approved by attorneys licensed in your state. Legal advice and any legal services you need, such as estate planning, wills, and healthcare directives, as well as probate and administration, are provided by independent licensed attorneys. You are never left to figure it out on your own.

02 What will this cost?

Final Affairs Assistance and Final Affairs Planning each have their own flat price, the same for every family. Families typically find both very affordable, and most families purchase right at the funeral home. That price covers your plan, your guidance center, and our support, and your service agreement spells out exactly what is included. Attorney services are separate and paid directly to the attorney; those fees depend on your unique situation and vary by scope of work, attorney, and location. Reach out and we will help you.

03 How do I get started?

Most families are introduced to us by their funeral home, which provides access and helps you set up your account. No account yet? No problem. Contact us directly and we will get you started.

04 I’m overwhelmed. Is that normal?

Completely. Most people have never done this before. That is exactly why Final Affairs exists. You bring what you know, and we will bring the order.

05 Will the estate need probate?

Not always. Some estates need no probate at all, some qualify for simplified probate, and some require the standard process. Finding out which applies is part of your Wind-Down Plan. Your plan gathers the facts and identifies which assets may pass outside probate, such as accounts with valid beneficiary designations or property titled with survivorship rights, and an independent attorney can confirm the answer in a short session, online or at their local office.

06 What is a Wind-Down Plan?

A step-by-step guide built from your answers: what to handle, in what order, and how. It covers identifying and documenting the estate, notifying the right institutions, and the path to closing everything properly. Because your plan is built from attorney-approved guidance for your state, it remains useful and ready to follow whether or not you choose to meet with an attorney. Final Affairs also offers tools that assist executors and administrators with the estate tasks that follow probate or administration.

07 Can I use Final Affairs before a loss?

Yes. Final Affairs Planning helps you put your own affairs in order ahead of time with an Advance Plan, easing the burden on your future personal representative. Ask us about it.

08 Who are the attorneys?

Attorneys help in two ways. Licensed attorneys approve the questions, guidance, and instructions the platform uses for your state. And when you want advice or legal services, you can have your plan reviewed by an independent attorney online or meet with a local attorney in person, for matters such as probate or an estate plan. You can have a consultation with an attorney anytime online to have your questions answered. All legal advice and legal services are provided by independent attorneys. Every attorney in our network is licensed, in good standing, experienced, and maintains a local office. Attorneys represent you directly, under their own engagement with you. Separate fees apply for all legal services.
