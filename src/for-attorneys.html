Join Our Network

Final Affairs is seeking experienced probate and estate planning attorneys for its network of independent local counsel. There is no fee to join, and every attorney must pass a diligence check. We accept only attorneys with a local office; virtual-only practices are not eligible.

Local attorneys only

We do not accept virtual-only practices. Attorneys offering probate or estate planning services must maintain a local office and identify the county or counties they serve. Offices must be within 25 miles of the clients they accept, with limited exceptions for rural areas.
