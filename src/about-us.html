Who we are

About Us

We guide families through what must be done after a death, so their loved one’s final affairs are handled properly. And we help families plan ahead, so the burden that one day falls on their loved ones is lighter.

Our origin

Final Affairs started with one simple question.

What happens to a family after the service ends?

Our founder spent more than two decades making legal and administrative processes simpler for ordinary people, and kept hearing the same question whenever a friend lost a loved one: what do I do now? The questions begin immediately, and decisions cannot wait. Yet the person left in charge is handed no map. The help that exists is incomplete and fragmented, and legal help often arrives months later, during probate. So our founder set out to solve it: the whole tangle of settling and planning for a person’s final affairs.

Final Affairs was built to close that gap, to be there the moment the questions start, with calm, organized answers and a clear plan to follow, all from one centralized guidance center.

Our mission

To bring calm, order and clarity to the final affairs process following the loss of a loved one, and to give families the opportunity to plan ahead and lessen the burden for the future.

Our values

What guides our work

Four commitments shape everything we do, for every family and every partner.

Partnership

We work alongside funeral homes, attorneys and other professionals so every family gets the right help from the right hands.

Clarity

Plain words, clear steps, no jargon. If it can’t be explained simply, we haven’t finished our job.

Stewardship

Families trust us with what matters most. We guard their information, their time, and their peace of mind.

Compassion

We meet every family in a hard season, and we act like it: patient, unhurried, human.

Trusted partners

Final Affairs works alongside funeral homes, licensed attorneys, and other professionals to give families clear guidance every step of the way.
